Stop. What you just said did not land. Do not patch it with one more clarifying sentence —
**re-pitch it from scratch**.

- **Throw the failed framing away.** Restating the same idea in more words is the failure mode. It
  was not too short; it was pitched wrong.
- **Open with the context the explanation assumed** — what the thing is, what it is for, and what
  problem it was answering.
- **Re-pitch under the cold-read rules** — one idea per sentence, active voice, the names the
  codebase and the team already use. If the failure was a term you coined, replace it; don't define
  it.
- **A term that failed once fails everywhere.** Grep it, and report every other site before I have
  to ask.
- **If the confusion is in a document, the fix belongs in the document** — name the lines you would
  change, don't just explain better in chat.
